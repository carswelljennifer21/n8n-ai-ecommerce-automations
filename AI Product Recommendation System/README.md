# 🛍️ AI Product Recommendation System

An AI-powered product recommendation automation built with **n8n and AI** that analyzes customer information and product data to generate relevant product recommendations for eCommerce customers.

## 🎯 Project Overview

eCommerce businesses have large product catalogs, which can make it difficult for customers to find products that match their interests and needs.

Generic product suggestions may not always be relevant to individual customers.

This automation demonstrates how AI can analyze customer and product information to generate more personalized product recommendations.

## 🚨 The Problem

Without personalized recommendations:

* Customers may struggle to find relevant products
* Businesses may miss cross-selling opportunities
* Product discovery can be less effective
* Manual recommendation processes are difficult to scale
* Customers may receive generic suggestions

## 💡 The Solution

This n8n workflow uses AI to analyze available customer and product information and generate relevant product recommendations.

The automation:

1. Receives customer information or preferences.
2. Processes the available product information.
3. Sends the relevant data to an AI model.
4. Analyzes the customer's needs or preferences.
5. Matches the customer with relevant products.
6. Generates personalized recommendations.
