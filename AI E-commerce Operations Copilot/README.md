# 🧠 AI E-commerce Operations Copilot

An AI-powered eCommerce operations automation built with **n8n and AI** that helps businesses analyze operational information, identify issues, generate insights, and support routine decision-making across eCommerce processes.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Running an eCommerce business involves managing many different operational tasks, including orders, inventory, customer requests, products, sales, and internal processes.

As a business grows, manually reviewing information and coordinating these tasks can become time-consuming.

This automation demonstrates how an AI-powered operations copilot can help businesses process operational information, identify potential issues, and provide useful recommendations from a centralized workflow.

## 🚨 The Problem

Growing eCommerce businesses often deal with:

* Large amounts of operational data
* Repetitive administrative tasks
* Manual information analysis
* Difficulty identifying issues quickly
* Time-consuming operational decisions
* Multiple processes that need to be monitored

Without automation, teams may spend significant time collecting information before they can determine what action needs to be taken.

## 💡 The Solution

This n8n workflow uses AI to act as an operations assistant that can analyze business information and provide useful operational insights.

The automation can:

1. Receive operational data or a business request.
2. Process the available information.
3. Send relevant information to an AI model.
4. Analyze the request or operational data.
5. Identify issues, patterns, or opportunities.
6. Generate recommendations or next steps.
7. Provide the result for business review or action.
