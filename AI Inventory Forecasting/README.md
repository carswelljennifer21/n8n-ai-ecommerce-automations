# 📦 AI Inventory Forecasting

An AI-powered inventory forecasting automation built with **n8n and AI** that analyzes inventory and sales data to help eCommerce businesses anticipate demand, identify potential stock shortages, and make smarter inventory decisions.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Managing inventory effectively is essential for eCommerce businesses. Having too much stock can increase storage costs, while having too little can lead to stockouts and missed sales.

This automation demonstrates how AI and workflow automation can analyze inventory data and provide forecasting insights to support more efficient inventory planning.

## 🚨 The Problem

Without an automated inventory forecasting process, businesses may face:

* Manual inventory analysis
* Unexpected stock shortages
* Missed sales opportunities
* Overstocking and unnecessary storage costs
* Difficulty identifying products that need restocking
* Time-consuming inventory planning

## 💡 The Solution

This n8n workflow uses AI to analyze inventory-related information and generate useful forecasting insights.

The automation:

1. Receives inventory and sales data.
2. Processes the available information.
3. Sends the relevant data to an AI model.
4. Analyzes inventory levels and demand patterns.
5. Identifies potential stock risks.
6. Generates inventory forecasting insights.
7. Provides recommendations to support restocking decisions.

## 🔧 Workflow

```text
Inventory & Sales Data
        ↓
Data Processing
        ↓
AI Analysis
        ↓
Demand & Stock Forecast
        ↓
Identify Inventory Risks
        ↓
Restocking Recommendation
```

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **n8n** — Workflow automation platform
* **AI/LLM** — Inventory analysis and forecasting
* **Data Processing** — Processes inventory and sales information

## 📋 Data Analyzed

The workflow can work with inventory information such as:

* Product name
* Current stock
* Sales data
* Historical demand
* Stock levels
* Inventory trends
* Forecasting information

## 📊 Example Scenario

An eCommerce business has a product with declining inventory and consistent sales.

The automation analyzes the available data and generates a forecasting insight:

```text
Product: Bluetooth Speaker
Current Stock: 25 units
Recent Sales: 8 units/week
Inventory Risk: Medium
Recommendation: Consider restocking soon
```

The workflow helps the business identify potential inventory issues before the product reaches a critical stock level.
