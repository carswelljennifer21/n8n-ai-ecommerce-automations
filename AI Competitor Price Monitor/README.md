# 💰 AI Competitor Price Monitor

An AI-powered competitor price monitoring automation built with **n8n and AI** that monitors competitor pricing data, compares product prices, and helps businesses identify important pricing changes and competitive opportunities.

## 🎯 Project Overview

eCommerce businesses operate in highly competitive markets where product prices can change frequently.

Manually checking competitor prices takes time and makes it difficult for businesses to react quickly to important pricing changes.

This automation demonstrates how n8n and AI can help automate competitor price monitoring and provide businesses with useful pricing insights.

## 🚨 The Problem

Without an automated competitor price monitoring process:

* Teams manually check competitor websites
* Important price changes can be missed
* Pricing analysis takes valuable time
* Businesses may react too slowly to market changes
* Maintaining competitive pricing becomes difficult

## 💡 The Solution

This n8n workflow automates the process of analyzing competitor pricing information.

The automation can:

1. Collect competitor product pricing data.
2. Process the available pricing information.
3. Compare competitor prices with the business's own prices.
4. Use AI to analyze important pricing differences.
5. Identify significant price changes or competitive opportunities.
6. Generate useful pricing insights for the business.

## 📋 Data Analyzed

The automation can analyze information such as:

* Product name
* Business price
* Competitor price
* Price difference
* Competitor name
* Product URL
* Price change
* Monitoring date

## 📊 Example Scenario

A business sells a Bluetooth Speaker for **₱2,199**.

A competitor's price changes from **₱2,299** to **₱1,999**.

The workflow detects the pricing change:

```text id="p3q4r5"
Business Price: ₱2,199
Competitor Price: ₱1,999
Price Difference: ₱200

        ↓

AI Analysis

        ↓

Competitor is now offering a lower price.
Review pricing strategy.
```

This allows the business to become aware of important competitor pricing changes without manually checking prices.
