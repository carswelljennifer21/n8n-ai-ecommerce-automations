# 📊 AI Sales Analytics Agent

An AI-powered sales analytics automation built with **n8n and AI** that processes sales data, analyzes business performance, identifies trends, and generates actionable insights to support better decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data, but manually reviewing that information can be time-consuming.

Sales teams and business owners need to quickly understand revenue performance, product trends, and areas that may require attention.

This automation demonstrates how AI can analyze sales data and turn raw information into useful business insights.

## 🚨 The Problem

Without automated sales analysis:

* Businesses spend time manually reviewing sales data
* Important sales trends can be overlooked
* Performance reporting can be repetitive
* Identifying underperforming products takes time
* Business decisions may be based on incomplete information

## 💡 The Solution

This n8n workflow uses AI to analyze sales information and generate useful business insights.

The automation:

1. Receives sales data.
2. Processes and organizes the available information.
3. Sends the data to an AI model for analysis.
4. Identifies important sales patterns and trends.
5. Highlights potential issues and opportunities.
6. Generates an easy-to-understand sales analysis.
